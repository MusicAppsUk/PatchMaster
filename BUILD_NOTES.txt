PatchMaster v21.16 — Federated Recording Recovery — 15 Sep 2026

ROOT CAUSE / RECOVERY
- v21.15 could still depend on Apple/iTunes returning a usable catalogue row before a recording became selectable.
- A real recording can therefore exist in MusicBrainz and PatchMaster research packets yet appear unrecognised when the Apple path fails or ranks poorly.

CHANGES
- Recording search is now federated: Apple Music + MusicBrainz + verified PatchMaster research packets.
- MusicBrainz is now a first-class title/artist recording resolver, not only an artist-catalogue fallback.
- Exact title score raised to dominate title expansions/covers with longer names.
- Verified research packets provide offline resilience for already-researched exact recordings.
- Search still requires user selection before research/build.
- Research and MODX M transport are otherwise inherited from v21.15.

REGRESSION CASE
- Somewhere Out There — Linda Ronstadt / James Ingram must be discoverable. It is present in MusicBrainz and the existing verified PatchMaster research packet.
